### B. Path-Specific Safe-Integer Reachable Range Derivations (Category 3)

There is no universal blanket input bound of 1e9 across the calculation engine. Each calculation module defines specific domain constraints and reachable ranges:

1. **Per-Unit Calculation (`per-unit-calc.ts`):**

   - **Reachable Ranges:** In per-unit calculations, input parameters are strictly bounded by Rule Pack limits. Across all synthetic and production Rule Packs, $minListPriceMinor \ge 99\text{¢}$, $maxListPriceMinor \le 100,000\text{¢}$ ($1,000.00), and $maxPages \le 2,000$. Maximum print cost per book is $85\text{¢} + 2,000 \times 1.2\text{¢} = 2,485\text{¢}$ ($24.85). Maximum delivery fee is $50\text{ MB} \times 15\text{¢} = 750\text{¢}$.
   - **Unreachability Argument:** Maximum gross royalty cannot exceed list price ($100,000\text{¢}$), and maximum unit cost cannot exceed $8,104\text{¢}$. Intermediate rational quantities and rounded integer amounts are bounded within $[-100,000, 100,000]$ cents—approximately 11 orders of magnitude below `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` ($9,007,199,254,740,091 \approx 9 \times 10^{15}$). The defensive overflow guards on L373, L400, L423, L453, and L475 are mathematically unreachable under valid Rule Pack configurations.

2. **Single Scenario Calculation (`scenario-calc.ts`):**

   - **Reachable Ranges:** Scenario inputs accept non-negative integer units and safe-integer list prices. Total revenue is calculated as $revenue = units \times listPriceMinor$.
   - **Exact-Boundary Acceptance:** The engine explicitly accepts exact safe-integer boundaries. At $listPriceMinor = 1\text{¢}$ and $units = 9,007,199,254,740,091$, $revenue = 9,007,199,254,740,091 = MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$. Test 34 in `scenario-calc.test.ts` executes this exact boundary without error, successfully killing mutant `1221` (`>= MAX_SAFE_INTEGER`).
   - **Overflow Rejection:** Any input where $units \times listPriceMinor > MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$ is strictly rejected with `CalcOverflowError` at the revenue boundary (L188). For example, at a minimum price of $99\text{¢}$, any unit count exceeding $\lfloor MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER / 99 \rfloor = 90,981,810,653,940$ immediately throws `CalcOverflowError`.
   - **Near-Boundary Coverage:** Checkpoint CHK-014 validates near-boundary scenario arithmetic ($4,503,599,627,370$ units at €20.00, yielding revenue $9,007,199,254,740,000$, exactly 91 below $MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$), proving precision is preserved near the boundary.
   - **Unreachability Argument for Intermediates:** Because revenue is verified $\le MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$ upfront, aggregate royalties (at most 100% of revenue) and aggregate costs cannot exceed $MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$. Since $grossRoyalty \ge 0$ and $totalCost \le MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$, net contribution $netContribution = grossRoyalty - totalCost \ge -MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER = MIN\_SAFE\_INTEGER$. Thus, downstream overflow checks on aggregate net contribution (L324–L325, L431–L432) are mathematically unreachable.

3. **Triad Scenario Calculation (`scenario-calc.ts`):**

   - **Reachable Ranges:** Triad scenario calculation executes three single scenarios (`low`, `base`, `high`), inheriting all single-scenario input validation and overflow rejection bounds.
   - **Ordering Logic:** All ordering comparisons (`priceOrdered`, `unitsOrdered`, `costVarianceOrdered`, `netContributionOrderingSatisfied`, `revenueOrderingSatisfied`) evaluate safe integer quantities verified during single-scenario execution.

4. **Break-Even Calculation (`break-even-calc.ts`):**

   - **Reachable Ranges:** `fixedCost.amountMinor` accepts safe integers $\ge 0$. When per-unit net contribution is positive ($netContribution > 0$), break-even units is computed as $\lceil fixedCost / netContribution \rceil$.
   - **Unreachability Argument:** For `breakEvenUnits` to approach $MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$, fixed cost must approach $MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$ with net contribution $1\text{¢}$. However, calculating break-even revenue ($breakEvenUnits \times listPriceMinor$) on L421 would immediately overflow safe integer bounds because $listPriceMinor \ge 99\text{¢}$ (or $\ge 1\text{¢}$). Negative underflow guards ($< MIN\_SAFE\_INTEGER$ on L407, L422) are mathematically unreachable because fixed cost $\ge 0$ and unit contribution $> 0$ strictly yield non-negative quotients.

5. **Sensitivity Calculation (`sensitivity-calc.ts`):**
   - **Reachable Ranges:** Evaluates single-parameter perturbations around a valid baseline scenario. Baseline scenario revenue is strictly bounded $\le MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$.
   - **Unreachability Argument:** Mutant `1740` guards against upper units exceeding $MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$ ($upperUnits = baseline.units + \Delta U > MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$). Any unit count approaching $MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$ cannot complete `calculateSensitivity` under valid Rule Packs because $minListPriceMinor \ge 99\text{¢}$, causing the perturbed single scenario to overflow scenario revenue ($units \times 99\text{¢} > MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$) and throw `CalcOverflowError`. For price perturbation (L319), list price is bounded by Rule Pack limits ($100,000\text{¢}$), making $MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER$ unreachable.
